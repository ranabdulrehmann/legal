## 1. Summary in Plain Language

We built QuickScan PDF to work entirely on your device. Here is the short version:

- The documents you scan, the images you capture, the PDF files the App generates, and the text extracted by optical character recognition (OCR) **never leave your device**. We do not upload them, we do not store them on any server, we cannot see them, and we never share them with anyone.
- We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the App.
- The App shows advertisements. The ad network (Google AdMob) collects device identifiers to serve those ads, as it would in any ad-supported free app.
- The App uses Google Firebase to collect anonymous usage statistics and automatic crash reports. This helps us fix bugs and improve the App.
- You can turn off personalised advertising and reset your advertising identifier at any time through your device's settings.

The rest of this policy explains all of this in detail.

## 4. Information the App Automatically Collects

Even though we do not collect your documents or your personal profile, some information is automatically collected when you use the App, in order to serve advertising, understand how the App is being used in aggregate, and diagnose crashes.

**A. Advertising identifiers and ad-related information (via Google AdMob):**

- Android Advertising ID (a resettable identifier assigned by your device);
- device information such as device model, operating-system version, screen size, language, general geographic location inferred from IP address (typically at the country or city level), mobile network carrier, and app version;
- information about the advertisements shown to you (such as which ads were requested, displayed, clicked, or dismissed);
- coarse timing information about your interactions with advertisements.

Google AdMob uses this information to select and deliver advertisements, measure their effectiveness, detect and prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you have not opted out, personalise the advertisements you see. See Section 8 for links to Google's privacy notices and instructions to opt out of personalised advertising.

**B. Analytics events (via Google Firebase Analytics):**

- anonymous usage events such as which screens are opened, which features are used, how often the App is launched, session duration, and similar aggregate signals;
- a randomly generated installation identifier that is not linked to your name, email, or Google account;
- device model, operating-system version, language, country inferred from IP address, and App version.

We use these signals only in aggregate to understand which features are used most, to guide improvements, and to detect problems. We do not attempt to identify individual users from analytics data.

**C. Crash and performance reports (via Google Firebase Crashlytics):**

- when the App crashes or encounters a serious error, an automatic report is sent containing the stack trace, the state of the App at the time of the crash, the device model, the operating-system version, and the App version;
- a randomly generated installation identifier;
- Crashlytics uses IP address to detect and prevent fraud and abuse; it does not retain IP addresses for longer than needed for that purpose.

Crash reports never contain the contents of your documents, images, PDFs, or extracted text.
